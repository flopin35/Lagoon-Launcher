Install in three steps

Lagoon isn't on an app store, so your tablet will ask before installing it. Open this page on the tablet itself.

  1. Download

    Tap Download APK. When the browser warns about the file type, choose Download anyway.

  2. Install

    Open the file from your downloads. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

  3. Make it home

    Press the Home button and pick Lagoon, or go to Settings › Apps › Default apps › Launcher and choose Lagoon.

To go back to your old home screen at any time, choose the original launcher in the same Default apps setting, or uninstall Lagoon.

What's inside

Small, quick, and gentle on a 2 GB tablet.

Dashboard home

Frosted glass tiles for the clock, live weather and forecast, now playing, quick controls, battery and storage, this month's calendar and your latest notifications.

Lagoon control centre

Swipe down for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, torch, do not disturb, rotation and more, brightness and volume sliders, music, and your notifications: tap to open, swipe to dismiss.

Lagoon lock screen

Wake the tablet to the Lagoon clock, weather, music and notifications, with torch and camera shortcuts. Swipe up, then your usual PIN keeps it safe.

A clock worth looking at

Choose an analog face with a ticking second hand, a glowing seconds ring, or big digits that roll into place. Pick its size and put it left or centre.

Every app, one swipe up

The app list reads your installed apps live and updates the moment you install or remove one. Search as you type.

Gestures

Swipe up for apps, swipe down for the control centre, long-press the home screen for settings, long-press an app for options.

Your wallpaper, your colours

Five drawn lagoon wallpapers, your device wallpaper, or any photo, on the home and lock screen. Six accent colours, with turquoise first.

Custom icons and names

Nine icon shapes, from squircle and circle to hexagon, flower and clover, in full colour or a soft mono tint, at any size. Rename any app.

Dock and pinned apps

Keep up to six apps in the floating dock and pin more to the home screen. Hide apps you never open.

Motion you choose

Gentle slides by default, a livelier bounce if you like it, or switch animation off entirely to save battery.

Tidefall
New game

Place blocks. Ride the falling tide.

A block puzzle made for tablets. Fill rows and columns to clear them; when a row clears, everything above it falls, and falling blocks can finish new lines in a chain reaction.

  • Chain reactions: each link multiplies your points, and big ones set off a MEGA COMBO.
  • Special blocks: bombs, lightning, rainbow and magnet pieces, plus rewinds you earn.
  • Survival: danger tides turn blocks to stone unless you clear them in time.
  • Daily Tide, Weekly Storm and friend challenges: everyone gets the same pieces, so it's a fair fight.
  • Upgrades and unlocks: pick one of three upgrades at milestones, earn XP, unlock themes and block styles.
